HypeHaus · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hypehaus Entertainment Private Limited ("HypeHaus", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the HypeHaus mobile application and related services (the "Service"). By using HypeHaus, you agree to the practices described here.

Who We Are

HypeHaus is operated by Hypehaus Entertainment Private Limited, a private limited company incorporated in India. You can reach us at hello@hypehaus.org.

Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information. Your phone number (verified via OTP) and date of birth (month + year only, used for age verification and birthday notifications).
  • Profile information. Display name, profile photo (optional), handle, and city.
  • Activity information. Events you've attended, tickets purchased, posts shared, ratings given, and connections made through the app.
  • Device information. Device model, operating system version, app version, and unique device identifiers necessary for security and authentication.
  • Location information. Approximate city-level location, derived from your selection or device settings. We do not track precise GPS location continuously.
  • Payment information. Payment details are processed by our payment partner Razorpay; we do not store full card numbers or banking credentials.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Service (authentication, ticket delivery, event discovery).
  • Verify your age and identity where required by law or by event organizers.
  • Process payments and refunds for ticket purchases.
  • Send notifications about events, tickets, and account activity.
  • Enable the social features of the app (verified reviews, connections made at events).
  • Improve the Service through aggregated analytics.
  • Comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud or abuse.

Age Requirement

HypeHaus is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with information, please contact us at hello@hypehaus.org and we will delete the relevant data.

How We Share Your Information

We share your information only as needed to provide the Service:

  • Event organizers. When you purchase a ticket, the event organizer receives your name, phone number, and ticket details necessary for entry verification.
  • Service providers. We use trusted third parties for SMS delivery (MSG91), payment processing (Razorpay), file storage (Cloudflare), and analytics (PostHog). These providers handle data only as needed to perform their service.
  • Other users. Your public profile, verified reviews, and event attendance may be visible to other users you've connected with at events.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of HypeHaus, our users, or others.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g., tax records for ticket purchases).

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent to marketing communications at any time.
  • Export your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@hypehaus.org.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, secure password hashing, and access controls limiting employee access to personal data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work continuously to protect your information.

International Transfers

Your information is processed primarily in India. If you use HypeHaus from outside India, your information may be transferred to and processed in India. By using the Service, you consent to this transfer.

Children's Privacy

HypeHaus is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the app or by email. Continued use of HypeHaus after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email us at hello@hypehaus.org.
